## 4. Caveats

- Classified from CLI JSON (`.review.finding[].title/detail/disposition/severity/status`); I did not open raw ledger TOML, `.doctrine/rfc/026/`, `.doctrine/slice/270/`, or QUE-224/225.
- I did not classify on any pre-existing `route`; the CLI JSON exposes no route field, and I ignored route wording inside dispositions/responses.
- Route was inferred from the *remedy the finding asks for*, not from whether the design claim is true. I did not verify findings against source.
- `none` was reserved for questions outside the five: plan/impact/revision completeness, and is labelled `neither`.
- The `control`/`probe` boundary is the largest source of error: a hostile input defeating a check could read either way (see RV-314 F-41, RV-307 F-32).
- The `review`/`owner-fix` boundary is the second: governance conflicts read as both records-disagreeing and commitment-adjudication.
- Some detail fields were read truncated (650–800 chars); classification there rests on title + the stated defect.
- RV-370 F-19 is a withdrawn near-duplicate of F-20 (identical detail, blank title); I counted it separately and routed it as F-20.
- Several dispositions are `null`/`design-wrong`/`descoped`; I routed the finding's question regardless of disposition outcome.
- Severities and counts were verified per ledger; no sampling, no unclassified remainder.
