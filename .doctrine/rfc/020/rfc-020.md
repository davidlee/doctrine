# RFC-020: Ledgered facet claims

## Context

RFC-019 Phases A–C are shipped (SL-210 capture, SL-213 inference, SL-217
elicitation queue); SL-219 (estimate comparison domain) is designed and ready.
The comparison ledger gave *relative* judgements full evidential discipline:
provenance, supersession, tombstones, concurrent contradiction surfaced as
findings. *Absolute* magnitudes — the `[value]` and `[estimate]` facets — have
none of it, and under REV-022's resolution policy they constitutionally
outrank the evidence layer built to calibrate them.

The SL-217 product critique named the two decisions most against the
subsystem's own grain:

- **§5** — one hand-typed float is constitutional truth while dozens of
  ledgered judgements are "evidence", contradicting the motivating claim that
  absolute scores are the weakest input;
- **§6** — provenance is modelled but epistemic authority is binary and
  equal: agent rows close what stakeholder rows close.

Both were accepted as *conscious transitional policies*. This RFC is the
transition.

The corpus reality makes it urgent (IMP-290, SL-219 elicit session): ~90% of
authored `value` and `estimate` facets were written by agents with no
provenance recorded. The resolver launders each guess into a point anchor
that overrides comparison projection — agent guesses out-rank the calibration
meant to correct them, and were the root cause of the quarantine knots
cleared in that session. Estimates are worse: `est_cost` is the *divisor* of
`value_dim`, so a guessed estimate scales the whole ranking, and no
calibration path exists at all until SL-219 lands.

This RFC consolidates IDE-039 (magnitude claims as ledgered evidence),
IMP-290 (provenance tier + resolver flip), RFC-019 T7/D7 (the agent-trust
demotion knob), IMP-281 (REQ value admissibility), IDE-013 (facet change
history), and the container-value / hierarchy-cascade questions raised in the
2026-07-16 discussion. It deliberates three coupled moves:

1. **Claims model** — absolute magnitude assignment becomes ledgered
   evidence, uniformly for value and estimates, with a tiered epistemic
   authority ladder replacing binary anchors-win.
2. **Hierarchy value** — REQs, PRDs, and spec containers join the value
   domain; containment semantics that don't lie.
3. **Governance routing** — the REV and spec-REQ changes the above require.

## Discussion

### T1 — the ontological move

**Value and estimates are not attributes of an entity; they are judgements
about it.** Judgements are events: they have an author, a date, a basis, a
context, and they can be superseded, contested, or withdrawn. An attribute
slot can hold none of that — every `value set` is a silent coup against the
previous number, and the context ("why 7.0?") dies in conversation history.
Estimates suffer this *more* than values: they are fallible, subjective
judgements under partial information, and the historical record — who
estimated, when, against what understanding of scope — is often more
informative than the current number.

End-state: entity files carry identity and scope; the comparison ledger
carries everything anyone has said about worth or cost — relative or
absolute, human or agent, provisional or pinned — as dated, attributed,
supersedable rows. No `[value]` or `[estimate]` facet table exists anywhere.

Surface consequences:

- `value set` appends an **anchor claim** row (subject, magnitude, rater,
  asserted_at, optional basis/note). `estimate set` appends the estimate
  equivalent (payload: range, skew, unit, confidence).
- `value pin` appends a **pin-admitted** claim through the operator-gated
  path (T3) — the explicit operator-policy override the critique asked
  `value set` to be honestly framed as.
- `value clear` appends a tombstone. Correction is supersession
  (`supersedes = <row-uid>`), never in-place edit.
- Resolution is one pure function: claims + comparisons + config →
  `AnchorMap` + bounds + projection. RFC-019 T3's source taxonomy
  (authored / projected / default) collapses into tiered claim resolution —
  no special-cased facet branch anywhere.
- `show` / `explain` always render value as derived-with-provenance:
  `6.2 — pin (rater, date, basis REQ-059)` vs
  `6.2 — projected, bounds (3.2, 9.1), 9 judgements`.

### T2 — one judgement interface (design invariant)

**Nothing in the row schema, authority ladder, supersession machinery, or
resolution seam is value-specific.** Domains differ only in payload type,
admissibility predicate, and constraint compiler. This is a stated invariant
the Phase design must preserve, not an aspiration:

- Row: `{uid, seq, subject(s), domain (value|estimate|risk), form
  (order|ratio|anchor), payload (typed per domain×form), rater
  (human|agent|migrated), lens?, basis?, note?, supersedes?, by?,
  asserted_at?, observed_at?}`. The
  new `form = anchor` is the single-subject absolute claim beside the existing
  two-subject `order`/`ratio`; it rides SL-210/213's wire, resolution
  (R-rules), and finding machinery unchanged — supersession, within-session
  revision, tombstones, cross-session concurrency all apply.
- **Authority is not a row column** *(RV-275 F-5: a free field would let any
  writer self-assert constitutional weight)*. Authority is *derived* from
  mandatory provenance plus the operator-gated pin admission (T3); the
  vocabulary is closed, and rows asserting contradictory provenance states
  are rejected at capture. `asserted_at` is when the judgement was made —
  stamped on every live-captured row, honestly absent on migrated rows;
  `observed_at` is when the system learned of it — stamped at migration,
  redundant (and omittable) at live capture (see *Migration*).
- A value anchor's payload is a magnitude (f64 — see T5). An estimate
  anchor's payload is the existing estimate facet shape. A risk domain slots
  in later with zero schema motion.
- Claim resolution becomes the upstream **builder** of the `AnchorMap` fed to
  `compile` — competing claims per item resolve to **zero or one** anchor
  (a conflicted tier may yield none, or disagreement bounds — T3) plus
  findings.
  SL-219's design (D-NF) pinned `AnchorMap` as the sole anchor seam precisely
  so this slots in without reshaping the constraint layer; SL-219 executes
  unchanged, before or in parallel with this work.
- Subject generality is deliberate: a claim's subject is *any admissible id*.
  The hierarchy work in T6 and the REQ seams in T7 ride the same rows with
  admissibility changes only.

### T3 — epistemic authority ladder and the resolver flip

Replace binary anchors-win with a tiered ladder:

> **pin** (explicit operator policy) > **human claim** > **comparison
> projection** > **agent claim** > **migrated/unknown claim** > **default**
> (`DEFAULT_VALUE`)

- The flip that matters (IMP-290): **agent-authored magnitudes become priors
  below projection**, not anchors above it. The human comparison ledger
  drives; agent guesses get calibrated instead of calcified. A pin retains
  the constitutional override — but is now a deliberate, attributed,
  auditable act, not a side effect of typing a number.
- **Pin admission is a contract, not a column** (RV-275 F-5). `pin` is
  mintable only through an explicit operator-gated admission path (the
  `value pin` verb under operator posture — the `worker_commit` gating
  precedent), never by row authorship; ordinary tiers derive from the row's
  mandatory `rater`. Demotion and contest are append-only supersession,
  never mutation.
- **Same-tier conflict never resolves silently** (RV-275 F-1). Two active
  claims at the same tier with incompatible magnitudes — two humans, or two
  pins — have no winner: not lexicographic, not latest-wins (RFC-019 T6
  forbids both for concurrent evidence). The conflict surfaces as a finding
  and a reprobe candidate; resolution is a human appending an explicitly
  superseding row. Whether a conflicted tier contributes nothing or
  contributes its disagreement interval as bounds is Phase 1 design-gate
  material (*Implementation path*); the posture constraints are fixed here —
  deterministic, surfaced, no invented winner, and no fall-through that lets
  an agent claim win *because* humans disagree.
- **Anchor claims obey the lens pooling discipline** (RFC-019 T5): the
  pooled fit consumes unlensed anchors only; lens-tagged anchors are
  captured losslessly and stay inert for `value_dim` until IDE-035. Full
  ladder × lens composition is OQ-2, bound to the Phase 1 gate.
- **Pins are governed.** A pin can be superseded, tombstoned, and contested;
  the SL-217 stale-anchor reprobe queue can nominate pin review and demotion
  with full history. Facet pins would only ever have git archaeology.
- **T7/D7 lands here, generalised.** The agent-trust demotion knob RFC-019
  made mandatory before stakeholder surfaces (Phase D+) is claim-resolution
  policy on this ladder — e.g. agent claims/comparisons excluded from
  *determinacy* (they propose orderings but never retire a question) — not a
  special case bolted onto comparisons.
- **Two confidence concepts, named apart** *(RV-275 F-4: the first draft
  declared confidence "derived, never authored" while carrying authored
  confidence% in the estimate payload — internally contradictory as
  stated)*. **Value-fit certainty** is derived — the tier-2 feasible
  bounds — and is never authored: "6.2, bounds (3.2, 9.1), 9 judgements, no
  pin" says more than a hand-typed 0.7 and cannot go stale. **Estimate range
  confidence** — the existing facet's confidence% — is not system confidence
  at all: it is the rater's self-reported uncertainty about their own range,
  part of the claim's *content* exactly like the magnitude. It survives
  migration, round-trips as payload, and its semantic force is unchanged
  from today: it parameterises the `est_cost` collapse (percentile
  framing) — evidence the engine consumes, never a certainty the engine
  asserts. System-level certainty for estimates (a feasible-region model) is
  Phase E's entry criterion, not this RFC's claim. No *system* confidence
  scalar is authored anywhere.

### T4 — versioning and RFC-015 synergy

Versioning falls out of the ledger form: append-only + explicit supersession
+ tombstones is a total version history — who changed the value, when,
superseding which prior claim, with the superseded claim still inspectable.
**IDE-013 dissolves** (value at the claims phase, estimates at theirs) with
no `[[estimate.history]]` unspec'd-residue sub-key ever existing. The schema
bump rides the ledger's existing version gate, additive.

RFC-015 (governance state on `refs/doctrine/gov`) both moots the one real
cost and rhymes with the choice: entity-file diffability of value edits stops
mattering when the authored corpus leaves the PR surface entirely, and an
append-only claim store riding a CAS'd linear governance WAL is the same
storage philosophy at two altitudes.

### T5 — magnitude precision

f64 stays. The SL-219 session's disputes were all 0.1-resolution false
precision (2.5 vs 2.8) — real churn, but there is meaningful signal between
2 and 3 in this corpus, so coarsening is **rejected for now** and noted as a
possible later *config gate*, with the retune coupling (kind weights,
`DEFAULT_VALUE` interplay) named so it isn't rediscovered.

### T6 — hierarchy value: admissibility, containment, cascade

**Admissibility is broad; pedigree is posture, not ontology.** REQs
(IMP-281), PRDs, and tech-spec containers join the value domain alongside the
existing work kinds. A2/A4 gate by domain commensurability and are untouched.
Requiring a PRD/REQ pedigree for value would be inconsistent (backlog and
slice values are admitted today with no REQ inheritance) and would leave
necessary work — bugfixes, risk mitigation, analytics, commercial
necessities — without valid representation. Instead, value-grounding
strictness is a **configurable posture** (`off | advisory | strict`):
advisory surfaces "un-grounded value claim" findings in `explain`/doctor;
strict warns at capture. Warn, never refuse — the REV-022 precedent.

**Judgements are level-scoped.** A claim or comparison speaks about its
subject *as scoped at its level* (RFC-019 T6's as-scoped identity, unchanged).
A container claim therefore already means *the packaged whole* — "PRD-004 vs
PRD-015, which matters more?" is the cheap, coarse, reliable stakeholder
judgement, admissible from day one. Cross-level comparisons (a member vs its
own container) are degenerate and refused at capture; same-level
cross-container comparisons are unremarkable.

**Containment carries no arithmetic in v1** *(revised at RV-275 F-2: the
first draft proposed a member-sum coherence diagnostic one paragraph after
declaring member value non-additive — the diagnostic smuggled in exactly the
aggregation rule it disclaimed)*. Value is not additive across members
(overlap, redundancy, complementarity, intent exceeding the enumerated REQs),
so *any* cross-level arithmetic — propagation or diagnostic — is unsound
until a per-container aggregation semantics is chosen and defended. V1
containment is therefore **capture-only**: claims at any level are recorded
losslessly and consumed at their own level; the only cross-level machinery is
the capture gate (a member vs its own container is refused as degenerate).
Cross-level coherence diagnostics arrive *with* the aggregation-mode decision
(OQ-1) and its ADR-018 revision, tested against packaged-feature,
partial-fulfilment, overlap, and multi-membership counterexamples — not
before.

**The consumption spine is v1 policy on a pluggable seam, not a model
commitment.** The v1 default: each decision context consumes value at exactly
one level (sequencing: work items from their own claims/projections, REQ
value reaching work only through the governed fulfils/burndown channel —
A1/A3 hold by construction; scoping/Phase E: the REQ graph is the decision
surface). But the packaged-feature case is a genuine counterexample to any
hard member-first rule — a coherent feature can carry all the value while its
members are worthless in isolation (half a checkout flow). The design must
therefore support:

- **Declarable aggregation mode per container** — *package-valued* (holistic;
  members individually near-worthless; value realised at closure delivery)
  vs *portfolio-valued* (members independent; container value a view).
  Burndown semantics differ per mode; fractional burndown of package value by
  delivered members is the apportionment trap — named here, not solved (OQ-1).
- **The cascade as named destination.** The end-state is work inheriting
  value *derived* from REQ/feature-level base — A1-consistent (elicit base at
  the container, derive consequence downward), and the engine already has the
  sibling pattern in leverage credit (value-denominated derived worth
  propagated through structure); a membership/fulfils channel is the same
  move on a different edge class. Because the claim store is level-agnostic,
  the consumption spine can migrate upward over time (authored per-slice
  value → derived-from-cascade) without touching a single stored row. That
  row-level future-proofing is the insurance this RFC actually buys;
  everything above it is revisable policy.

**Axis discipline.** The membership axis (`members.toml`) is the value
channel. The C4 `parent` chain and `descends_from` are architecture and
realisation — not value channels; tech work inherits priority through the
work graph, not spec lineage.

**Multi-membership** is safe by construction in v1: containment transfers
nothing, so a REQ in two specs double-counts nothing. Container-level
aggregate views over overlapping memberships are labelled as views and never
summed across containers into a score.

**The carrot — explicitly deferred with the arithmetic:** REQ-level value +
fulfils/burndown could yield container remaining-value views ("PRD-014: 60%
of member value delivered") — the derived, never-authored progress surface
the scoping story wants. But a percent-delivered view is a member sum, so it
rides *behind* the aggregation-mode decision (portfolio mode at minimum),
not in v1 (RV-275 F-2 applies to it as much as to coherence checks).

### T7 — REQ provenance, confidence, and the backwards lifecycle (awareness)

Out of scope, but the seams are deliberate:

- The claims interface **is** the provenance/confidence pattern REQs adopt
  later — subject generality means REQ-targeted claims (worth, settle-cost,
  eventually confidence-in-the-requirement-itself) need no schema change.
- REQs will need their own lifecycle extending *backwards* into the
  pre-cognitive phases of product design. HYP → REQ graduation with
  EVD-backed confidence (PRD-010, SL-159) is one valid transition; others are
  expected — e.g. *stated-need → backsolved problem → workable options*: "this
  is what they said they need; the actual problem behind it must be recovered
  and options explored before anything graduates to a requirement." An
  elicited assertion is evidence about the problem space, not yet a
  requirement.
- `spec req status` is a free any→any transition today; a governed state
  machine over an extended lifecycle is the later move. Nothing in this RFC
  precludes it, and the claims ledger gives those future states an evidence
  substrate rather than a new one.

### Migration: import, never discard — and never invent provenance

Existing `[value]`/`[estimate]` facets bulk-import as anchor claims with an
explicit **`migrated` provenance class** *(revised at RV-275 F-3: the first
draft imported everything as `rater = agent` dated at migration — replacing
missing provenance with false provenance, silently demoting any genuine
human judgement, and passing a synthetic conversion date off as a judgement
date)*. The import asserts only what is true: the magnitude existed in the
facet; who judged it, and when, is unrecorded. Mechanics:

- `rater = migrated` — unknown origin, ranking at the bottom of the evidence
  ladder, below *attributed* agent claims;
- **observed-at ≠ asserted-at**: the row carries the migration date as
  observed-at (conversion metadata); the judgement date is honestly absent.
  Where defensible, the facet's git commit date/author ride along as `basis`
  evidence — recoverable context, never asserted provenance;
- import is **idempotent** and **census-verified**: every facet accounted
  for, zero silent provenance conversions (human-to-agent or otherwise),
  lossless rollback — each row cites its source facet, and the facet tables
  retire only after the census passes at each domain's conversion phase.

Humans **re-assert deliberately** (a pin or a fresh human claim) where they
mean it. This strictly dominates discarding: the estimate denominator — which
nearly every item's score depends on — survives, correctly ranked as
unattributed, and nothing is lost.

### Governance routing

- **REV against ADR-015**: dissolves REV-022's anchors-win posture into
  tiered claim resolution (the value-source policy rewrite). Rides the
  Phase 1 design gate.
- **ADR-018 untouched in v1**: containment is diagnostic-only, so burndown
  semantics don't change until aggregation modes land (their own later REV).
- **Spec REQs**: claim schema meaning/reader/retention into SPEC-020 and
  PRD-014 (the route IDE-013 itself prescribed); resolver policy into
  PRD-011/SPEC-001's descent.

### Implementation path

Slice-sized phases, strictly additive, each independently shippable:

- **Phase 0 — baseline diagnostic** (IMP-290's cheap diagnostic): force
  values to default / zero the value coefficient, diff top-N ranking against
  live. Evidence for the flip and the regression baseline. Trivial, first.
  *Delivered by SL-220 (2026-07-17; `scripts/value_baseline.py`, phase0-*
  evidence in the slice dir; RV-277).*
- **Phase 1 — value claims.** **Gate obligation** (RV-275 F-1/F-5): the
  complete active-claim algebra — row identity, closed derived-authority
  vocabulary, pin admission, supersession, same-tier concurrency, lens
  participation, deterministic conflict handling — designed and proven with
  permutation, duplicate-merge, cross-session, conflicting-pin, and
  lens-isolation tests before implementation. Then: `form = anchor` (schema
  bump on the version gate); claim resolution as `AnchorMap` builder;
  resolver flip per the T3 ladder; `value set|pin|clear` re-plumbed to
  claim-emitting; migration import per the census contract; `explain`/`show`
  provenance rendering; demotion-knob config. The ADR-015 REV rides this
  phase's design gate.
  *Delivered by SL-220 (2026-07-17; gate obligation discharged by the §8.3
  battery before the flip; ADR-015 REV = REV-024 approved pre-flip, applied
  at PHASE-07; 185-facet migration census 185/185/0/0; RV-277). The RFC
  stays open for Phases 2–3.*
- **Phase 2 — estimate claims.** After SL-219 lands: estimate anchor payload,
  import per the census contract, `est_cost` resolution through claims,
  `[estimate]` facet retires.
- **Phase 3 — hierarchy.** REQ/PRD/SPEC admissibility (folds IMP-281,
  including value-bearing-without-frontier-membership); pedigree posture
  config; cross-level capture gate. **No cross-level diagnostics** (RV-275
  F-2) — those ride the later aggregation-modes seam with its ADR-018 REV.
- **Later seams, named and gated:** inferential cascade + aggregation modes
  (+ ADR-018 REV); per-audience and as-of resolution surfaces; magnitude
  coarsening config; the REQ lifecycle (T7).

Sequencing against RFC-019: SL-219 executes first or in parallel (unchanged).
Phase 1 delivers the trust policy RFC-019 makes mandatory before its Phase D
stakeholder surfaces, so this RFC is on Phase D's critical path.

### Rejected framings

- **Provenance tier as facet columns** (IMP-290's minimal form). Keeps two
  ontologies; no versioning, no counterfactuals, no concurrent-claim
  semantics; risks the unspec'd-residue pattern IDE-013 warned about.
- **Authored facet survives as the pin surface** (this discussion's own
  earlier recommendation). Rejected for conceptual purity: it preserves the
  split ontology, exempts precisely the highest-authority tier from
  governance (supersession, contest, reprobe), and its remaining argument —
  entity-local PR diffability — is mooted by RFC-015.
- **Discard corpus values at migration.** Import-as-migrated-claims strictly
  dominates: keeps signal, honest (absent) provenance, correct ranking.
- **Authored confidence scalar.** Bounds are derived confidence; a hand-typed
  0.7 is another un-provenanced number to govern and goes stale silently.
- **Pedigree as ontology** (product-axis-only container value, REQ-grounding
  required). Inconsistent with admitted backlog/slice values; leaves
  necessary un-pedigreed work unrepresentable. Demoted to configurable
  posture.
- **Inferential containment from day one.** Unsound without a chosen and
  defended aggregation semantics; v1 ships capture-only containment and the
  pluggable consumption seam instead.

## Outcome

Open. Position taken: absolute magnitude assignment joins the comparison
ledger as first-class evidence under one domain-parameterised judgement
interface; epistemic authority becomes a tiered ladder (pin > human claim >
projection > agent claim > migrated claim > default), derived from provenance
and operator-gated pin admission, with pins as governed rows; value-fit
certainty is derived as bounds, never authored (rater-stated estimate range
confidence is claim content, distinct); versioning is supersession; existing
facets import under an explicit `migrated` provenance class; REQs/PRDs/spec
containers join the value domain with capture-only containment, a pluggable
consumption spine, and configurable pedigree strictness; estimates are
co-equal by construction.

Adjudicated in-discussion (2026-07-16, operator + agent): pins-as-ledger-rows
over facet-as-pin (purity chosen with eyes open on hand-editability);
magnitude coarsening rejected for now; import-not-discard migration; broad
admissibility with configurable pedigree; one-spine + capture-only
containment demoted to v1 default policy on a pluggable seam (the
packaged-feature counterexample); single RFC covering claims + hierarchy +
routing.

Adjudicated:

- **RV-275** *(2026-07-16, inquisition on the first draft)* — five findings,
  all accepted and integrated in this revision. F-1 (blocker): same-tier and
  lens-scoped anchor conflict resolution was undefined at the exact seam that
  produces one `AnchorMap` entry → posture fixed in T3 (deterministic,
  surfaced, no invented winner, no fall-through), complete active-claim
  algebra bound to the Phase 1 design gate with named test obligations. F-2
  (blocker): the member-sum coherence diagnostic contradicted the RFC's own
  non-additivity premise → all cross-level arithmetic (diagnostics *and* the
  burndown-view carrot) deleted from v1 and gated on OQ-1's aggregation
  contract + ADR-018 REV; v1 containment is capture-only. F-3: migration
  fabricated agent authorship and judgement dates → explicit `migrated`
  provenance class, observed-at/asserted-at split, idempotent census-verified
  import with lossless rollback. F-4: authored estimate confidence
  contradicted the never-authored doctrine → two concepts named apart
  (derived value-fit certainty vs rater-stated range confidence as claim
  content with unchanged `est_cost` force). F-5: a free `authority` column
  let any writer self-assert constitutional weight → authority removed from
  the row and derived from provenance + operator-gated pin admission;
  demotion is append-only supersession. Consequence: Phases 1 and 3 ship
  only through their stated gates.

Open questions:

- **OQ-1** Aggregation-mode vocabulary and burndown semantics for
  package-valued containers — including the apportionment question (does
  package value burn only at closure delivery, and what does partial delivery
  render as?). Gates the ADR-018 REV **and all cross-level diagnostics and
  container progress views** (RV-275 F-2).
- **OQ-2** Ladder × lens interaction: how authority tiers compose with
  lens-partitioned rows and future per-audience resolution (OQ-1 of
  RFC-019). Pooling posture is fixed (unlensed anchors only, T3); the full
  composition is Phase 1 gate material (RV-275 F-1).
- **OQ-3** REQ value-bearing without frontier membership — mechanism (kind
  weight, frontier filter, or context predicate) and its `survey`/`next`
  rendering. Phase 3 design material.
- **OQ-4** Whether `incomparable`-style abstention needs an anchor-claim
  analogue ("cannot estimate / cannot value now") as selector fodder.
