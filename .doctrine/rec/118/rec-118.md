# REC REC-118 — accept

Reconciliation record (SPEC-002). The structured deltas + evidence live in the
sister `rec-NNN.toml`; this prose companion carries the rationale for the act.

## Rationale

`doctrine slice status 265 done` refused with undischarged status-lag drift on
`REQ-482` (authored: `active`). The divergence is exactly the one design sec-5
`EX-6` plans for: `spec req add` mints a requirement `pending`, and the flip to
`active` happens at reconcile, *after* the coverage cell was recorded — so the
requirement's authored status and its scanned coverage composite part company
until an act records the move (`REQ-113`).

This REC is that act, owned by the closing slice. It carries the applied fact
(`REQ-482` `pending → active`) and the one coverage key feeding the composite —
the check-bound `VT` cell in `.doctrine/slice/265/coverage.toml`, whose bound
check is `tests/e2e_show_equivalence.rs` and which
`doctrine coverage verify 265` re-derives to `verified`.

Not a `revise`/`redesign`: no instance truth is contradicted — the evidence
exists and the authored status now agrees with it. The gate that flagged it is
discharged by recording agreement, not by changing any requirement.
