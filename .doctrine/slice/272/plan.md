## Overview

Two phases, one per design section: `PHASE-01` the refusals (design sec-2:
`ISS-482`, `IMP-499`), `PHASE-02` the change rows (design sec-3: `ISS-454`,
`ISS-488`). Design verification criteria are cited as "design VT-n"; each
phase's own VT ids are local to it.

## Sequencing & Rationale

The two halves share no code path, so the order is chosen by risk. Refusals
first: they touch the wire seam (`Batch::validate`) and the published
contract, and a regenerated contract is an outward artefact worth settling
early. Change rows second: they touch the apply spine and the change-log
vocabulary, and the `REQ-478` roster test is the last thing to go green.

Each phase is small enough for one TDD cycle per criterion; splitting further
would buy only handoff cost.
