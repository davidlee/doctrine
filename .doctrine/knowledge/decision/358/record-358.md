An arm is a candidate pool paired with a ranker. Pools: BM25 top-N (`doctrine search -p`), or the whole snapshot (entity bodies, DEC-357). Rankers: `bm25` (search order); `point` (batched Noul per section, DEC-352 packing, DEC-355 rubric); `list` (one Choice question whose options are the candidates, ranked by option probability). Arms: A = BM25 pool / bm25; B-point; B-list; C-point; C-point then list over its top ~100 (the snapshot cannot be one Choice: 255-option cap, `raw/jev-api.md:125`, and the pool is one question inside the 32k state+question budget). Constraints carried from the Hindsight evidence: no 'none of these' option or 'nothing relevant' level (it wins on hard queries); Choice probabilities are only comparable within one call, so list output is used as an order, never as a cross-pool score; the trial ranks, it does not filter. Evidence: Hindsight 0.10.1 (third-party blog, LoCoMo) measured listwise Choice recall@1 0.94 vs 0.87 for per-candidate Noul on the same model, at 1/30 of the calls. Refines DEC-352 (whose 'one shape for B and C' now holds per ranker; its packing check stays as a sanity check) and DEC-355 (the Noul rubric now governs the point ranker only).
