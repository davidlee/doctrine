A shipped asset (install/, memory/, plugins/) may ground a claim on: (i) prose that stands without a reference; or (ii) a published logical address under reference/<name>.md. A repo-private entity id or file path is never permissible, at any tier. Repo-private ids do not dangle in a client repo - they silently resolve to the client's own unrelated record (ISS-309). Inline is the default for a one-clause rationale; a published address is used where the referent is corpus-internal and durable; publishing a new reference doc is the destination for reasoning with no shipped home. A widened citation vocabulary is rejected: any durable referent a client needs is either inlined or published.
