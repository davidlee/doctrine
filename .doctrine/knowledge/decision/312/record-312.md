The permissible-grounding rule (DEC-311) is project-global: it governs all future shipped authoring, not only SL-267. Per ADR-023 tier-3 content must be delivered through framework surfaces, and a knowledge DEC is tier-5 evidence that cannot bind future authors. So SL-267 authors a new ADR descending from ADR-005 (knowledge tiering) and ADR-019 (asset policies). The rule itself is delivered in shipped reference docs/skills; the ADR is its governance home.
