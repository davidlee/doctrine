PRD-017 OQ-1. The published library (RFC-021 C1 / Contract B) exposes
framework-owned material for inspection and copy. It is undecided whether the
shipped **memory corpus** belongs in that published set, or whether publication
covers only templates, hymns, curated reference, and reusable scripts/modules.

The corpus is *already* exposed for **search** and **retrieval** through its own
native surface; publishing it **for copy** through the library is a distinct
classification decision, entangled with licence (owned-internal vs copyable, and
OQ-2's "looks copyable but encodes owned process logic" edge).

**Deferred deliberately in SL-223** (the publication-seam slice): SL-223 builds the
mechanism and ships a **templates-only** provisional declaration (all MIT,
customizable). The collection-set decision waits for the slice that actually
consumes it — the `library list|tree|show` read surface. Settling this blocks the
published library's initial collection set and how far licence classification
reaches.
