# IMP-496: Trial human engagement guidance in audit and reconciliation

## Aim

Trial RFC-034's shared calibration model with prose changes before considering
typed settings or CLI mechanisms. This project's default is a whiteboard-level
account of consequential features, with verbal changes to explanation and
participation during a session. The human keeps decision authority; no
understanding test or extra acceptance act is added.

## First pass

- Put the project default in `.doctrine/governance.md` and the same editable
  starter in the published `install/governance.md` template. A project's own
  governance file, not the template, is loaded by `doctrine boot`.
- Add a short audit handoff through systems, an important behaviour, evidence,
  and remaining risk; include exact read commands for consequential Doctrine
  sources. Offer deeper reading, useful human judgment, or one grounded mock
  peer challenge when appropriate. New defects still go to the RV ledger.
- At consequential reconciliation changes, explain the current and proposed
  text and its future effect. Offer a walkthrough within the existing
  confirmation or REV approval interaction, without another gate.
- Keep the framework skill guidance responsive to each project's governance
  default and verbal session changes. Leave design-time inquiry, typed
  configuration, and browser/editor opening (IDE-059) outside this task.

## Trial and completion

Try the guidance on a slice with a consequential audit concern and a real
reconciliation decision. Record which reading route or invitation the human
used or declined, whether their input changed a risk account or governing text,
and where the interaction was repetitive or opaque. A single slice is a
usability probe, not proof of lasting understanding. Close this item after the
prose is checked and that pilot observation is recorded; adjust the guidance if
the trial exposes a clear problem.
