Two blind spots, distinct in kind

Inter-unit · in the ratchet

Acyclic edges are free

count_tangle_edges only counts edges inside non-trivial SCCs. 35 of 149 command↔command edges are acyclic — they contribute zero. Fan out along any DAG tail and the number never moves. Concentration can also shift silently: drop one SCC edge, add another, stay ≤ baseline.

Intra-unit · below granularity

The collapse hides a whole star

extract_edges folds src/commands/*.rs into one unit. commands::cli's 15-way fan-out over its siblings becomes a dropped self-edge. Sub-module coupling inside any top-level unit is invisible by construction — not a hole in the ratchet, beneath it.

Reading the numbers

The big numbers are mostly the architecture working. High out-degree into pure leaves is the thin-shell shape ADR-001 wants, not rot. commands (31/2) is a dispatch source — nothing depends back on it; a router fans out. The healthy sinks confirm the layering: listing, entity, git each pull 25+ inbound with zero outbound.

The real signal is what the gate can't measure. The tangle ratchet is one integer — it can't see that integrity is a symmetric 13/13 nexus, that 35 edges are structurally free, or that a 15-way star hides one collapse below. No active rot today; the risk is that it could accrue while the gate stays green.

What to do — capture, don't gate

  1. Baseline & watch. This graph is the reference snapshot. Building a fan-out gate now invites false-positive churn with no evidence of harm.
  2. Define the trigger. Revisit if command tangle stalls near 123 across N slices, or a single unit's same-tier fan-out crosses a threshold — mirroring ADR-001's own "promote engine to a crate when churn settles" escalation.
  3. Cheap visibility if it fires. Promote dump_real_graph from #[ignore] to a committed snapshot with same-tier-filtered degree — a report, not a ratchet.
