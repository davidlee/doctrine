An open blocking question is a standing admission that the design might be
wrong in a way that matters. Carrying one past this point does not resolve it;
it makes it invisible.

The word that misleads is *disposed*. It does not mean answered. A question
can be answered, withdrawn as mistaken, deferred with a reason, or accepted as
a risk somebody is choosing to carry — all of those are dispositions, and the
last two are legitimate. The only outcome not available is leaving it silent.

The move to be honest about is re-grading a question as non-blocking so this
clears. Sometimes that is the right call and the reasoning behind it is real.
When it is done to get past this point, the design keeps the risk and loses
the record of it, which is strictly worse than never having raised the
question — and it degrades what this means for every design after it.
