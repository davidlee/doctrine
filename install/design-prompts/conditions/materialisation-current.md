The run carries a record of what the design says; the document is what people
actually read. When the two drift apart, every judgement made against the
record — every review, every acceptance — is a judgement about a text that no
longer exists.

This gets mistaken for bookkeeping, a save step to be performed at the end. It
is not. It is what makes every other guarantee refer to something. The related
error is editing the document by hand and expecting the run to catch up on its
own; it cannot, and it will not guess. All it can do is compare what it was
told against what is there now and report that the two differ.

Left stale, the cost lands on everyone downstream rather than on whoever left
it. A reader is told the design was reviewed and cannot find out what was
reviewed, which is a worse position than being told nothing.
